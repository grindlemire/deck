# Sources

Put the raw material for this talk next to this file: your notes, transcripts of voice memos, posts, papers, and a links.md of URLs. Then have Claude write this digest: each fact, number, quote and example the talk might use, numbered, said the way the source says it, with where it came from.

Cite items from the outline (`Sources: S1.2`) or a slide (`data-src="S1.2"`). `Kind: own` is your own work and gets no credit on screen. `Kind: other` is someone else's: the deck credits it on the slide that cites it and on the credits slide.
